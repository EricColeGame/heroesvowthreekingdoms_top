## Understanding How Recruitment Works

Recruitment in Heroes Vow: Three Kingdoms follows the familiar collection-RPG loop: spend a currency, receive a general, and convert duplicates into star upgrades or shards. What separates it from similar games is how tightly the campaign gates progress behind specific roles.

| Recruitment Outcome | What You Get | Long-Term Value |
|---|---|---|
| New general (unique) | Unlocks a roster slot and skill set | High — expands team options |
| Duplicate general | Star-up material or shards | High for your core team, low for benched units |
| Faction shards | Partial progress toward a specific hero | Medium — slow but targeted |
| Fodder units | Upgrade material for other heroes | Low individually, useful in bulk |

The key insight: a duplicate of a general you actually field is often worth more than a brand-new general you will never level. Community reports consistently show that players who funnel resources into a tight core of five to six heroes out-progress players who spread thin across twenty.

### Star Levels Beat Roster Width

A three-star epic that fits your faction synergy usually outperforms a one-star legendary sitting alone with no support. Before you chase a shiny new unit, ask whether you can realistically star it up within the next few weeks.

### Recruitment Is a Marathon, Not a Sprint

Every banner eventually rotates back. Missing a rate-up is not a catastrophe — it is a scheduling problem. Save, wait, and pull when the odds and your roster needs align.

## Recruitment Currencies and Where to Get Them

Knowing what each currency is for prevents the classic mistake of burning premium tokens on a standard pool.

| Currency | Typical Source | Best Use | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premium recruitment tokens | Events, achievements, guild rewards | Rate-up banners with a hero you need | Highest |
| Standard tickets | Daily quests, campaign milestones | Free daily pulls, filler banners | Medium |
| Faction-specific items | Faction dungeons, shop exchanges | Targeting a missing role (tank, healer) | Medium-High |
| Guild shop currency | Guild boss participation | Shards for core-team heroes | High |
| Event currency | Limited-time events | Exclusive heroes before they rotate out | Highest |

A simple rule that holds up across most accounts: never spend premium currency on a banner where you would be happy with fewer than half the featured heroes. If the pool is mostly filler for your roster, skip it.

### Daily and Weekly Income

Log in daily even when you have no time to play. Daily quests, idle rewards, and guild check-ins are the steady drip that funds your next big pull. Missing three days of dailies can cost you a full ten-pull over a month.

### Event Currency Is Time-Limited

Event shops close. If an event offers a hero you cannot get anywhere else, prioritize that currency above everything else. Limited heroes frequently do not return for a long time, and community experience suggests waiting for a rerun is a gamble.

## Heroes Vow Three Kingdoms Recruitment Strategies That Work

These are the habits that separate efficient accounts from frustrated ones.

### 1. Build a Wishlist Before You Pull

Write down the three to five heroes your team is missing — usually a dedicated tank, a healer, and a damage dealer that matches your main faction. Then only pull when a banner features at least two of them.

### 2. Track Your Pity Progress

Most gacha systems guarantee a high-rarity pull after a set number of attempts. Check the banner info screen for the exact counter rather than trusting forum numbers, which vary by banner type. Never let a nearly-full pity counter expire on a banner you do not care about — finish it or bank it deliberately.

### 3. Compare Banner Types Before Committing

| Banner Type | Rarity Odds | Best For | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard pool | Fixed, always available | New players filling empty slots | Low value at high level |
| Rate-up (featured) | Boosted for specific heroes | Chasing a known meta pick | Medium — off-banner pulls happen |
| Faction-limited | Restricted to one faction | Completing a synergy team | High if you lack that faction |
| Beginner / newcomer | Discounted or guaranteed | First week of a new account | Expires, so use it early |

### 4. Pull in Batches, Not Singles

If a banner offers a bonus for ten-pulls — extra shards, a guaranteed rare, or mileage points — batch your pulls. Singles are for finishing a pity counter, not for routine spending.

### 5. Stop When You Hit Your Target

The hardest discipline in heroes vow three kingdoms recruitment is walking away after a win. Once you land the hero you wanted, bank the rest. Chasing a second copy on the same banner usually means missing the next one.

## Faction Synergy and Roster Balance

Faction bonuses are where raw stats turn into real damage. Most team compositions reward stacking two or three factions rather than fielding five unrelated legends.

| Faction Archetype | Typical Strength | Pairs Well With | Watch Out For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggressive / damage-focused | Fast clears, burst damage | Control or debuff support | Folds against sustained fights |
| Defensive / tank-heavy | Survives long boss fights | Healers and scaling damage | Slow campaign clears |
| Balanced / hybrid | Flexible in most modes | Any secondary faction | Rarely the best at anything |
| Control / utility | Shuts down enemy skills | High-damage carries | Needs investment to shine |

When you plan a pull, think about which slot the new hero fills. A second top-tier damage dealer adds less to your account than the healer you have been missing for two weeks.

### Role Coverage Checklist

Before any major pull session, confirm you have:

- At least one reliable frontline unit that can absorb damage
- One healer or shield provider for extended fights
- One primary damage dealer at a competitive star level
- One utility hero for debuffs, stuns, or energy control
- A backup damage type in case a boss resists your main one

If two or more boxes are empty, target banners that fill them rather than chasing raw rarity.

## FAQ

**Is there a working recruitment code for Heroes Vow: Three Kingdoms?**
Promo codes circulate periodically through official social channels and community hubs, but they expire quickly and vary by region. Redeem anything you find immediately, and never trust a code that asks for account credentials. For heroes vow three kingdoms recruitment purposes, codes are a bonus — not a strategy.

**How many pulls until a guaranteed legendary?**
Pity thresholds differ by banner type, so check the in-game banner details for the exact counter. Community reports often quote numbers that apply to one specific banner and do not carry over to others.

**Should free-to-play players pull on every banner?**
No. Free-to-play accounts should skip most banners and concentrate on rate-ups that fill a genuine roster gap. Saving across two or three banners typically yields better results than spreading pulls thin.

**Do duplicate heroes really matter that much?**
Yes. Star upgrades from duplicates are usually the single largest power increase available to a hero, and they cannot be bought with time alone. Prioritize duplicates for heroes already in your main team.

**What if I pull a hero I do not need?**
Keep them. Roster depth matters for faction-locked modes and future synergy shifts. Just do not invest upgrade materials until they earn a permanent slot.

## Final Thoughts

Heroes Vow Three Kingdoms recruitment rewards patience far more than luck. Set a target, track your pity counter, respect faction synergy, and walk away when you win. Do that consistently and your roster will compound — while impulsive players keep starting over.
